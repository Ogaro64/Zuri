Our Story

Cooking From the Heart of Kenya

Zuri Kitchen was born in a small jiko in Westlands, Nairobi — where Mama Zuri cooked for neighbors who kept coming back for more. Today, we serve hundreds daily, but the soul of every dish remains the same.

We source ingredients fresh from local markets every morning. No shortcuts. No compromises. Just real food, real fire, real flavor.
